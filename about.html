The LUMORA Heritage

CREATED WITH PURPOSE. DESIGNED TO ILLUMINATE.

LUMORA was born from a singular passion: to liberate luxury lighting from generic mass manufacturing, uniting time-honoured European artisanal glassmaking with daring architectural geometry.

Our Narrative

THE EVOLUTION OF AN ILLUMINATION HOUSE.

Founded by an architect and a third-generation glassmaster, LUMORA emerged in response to an unfulfilled need in high-end interior architecture.

Too often, lighting was treated as an afterthought—an ornamental fixture selected from catalogues after spaces were already complete. We believed that illumination should be conceived as the heart of architectural volume, shaping spatial perception, comfort, and emotional resonance.

Today, from our Mumbai flagship showroom and European fabrication partner ateliers, we design and produce bespoke chandeliers, kinetic pendants, and architectural fixtures for landmark estates, luxury hospitality, and international private residences.

Ethos & Principles

THE BALANCE OF STATUESQUE AND SUBTLE.

Every LUMORA fixture is guided by four immutable design tenets.

01

Scale in Harmony

We reject both arbitrary oversized showpieces and meek undersized fixtures. Proportion must respect room volumes and ceiling elevations.

02

Tactile Authenticity

No plastics, faux-finishes, or hollow stampings. If it looks like heavy solid brass, it is solid brass. If it shines like crystal, it is precision optical glass.

03

Glare-Free Comfort

Light must welcome, never blind. High-output diodes are carefully diffused through opal glass, fluted prisms, or deep optical snoots.

04

Generational Life

Modular LED engines and standardized drivers mean LUMORA chandeliers are completely serviceable and built to endure for decades.

Atelier Pedigree

AN ALLIANCE OF ARTISAN HANDS.

While our engineering team models structural stresses and photometric distribution digitally, the soul of each piece is shaped by human hands.

In our partner glassworks, blowers rotate molten gathers on blowpipes, trusting their muscle memory and breath to shape consistent flutes. In our metal finishing bays, craftsmen apply wire-brushing, chemical patina baths, and hand-rubbed wax seals.

The result is a subtle kinetic organic presence—light fixtures that feel alive, possessing micro-variations that distinguish authentic luxury from industrial uniformity.

Technical Rigour

PHOTOMETRIC EXCELLENCE MEETS SCULPTURE.

A chandelier should look breathtaking turned off during noon sunlight—and cast flawless, soothing, shadowless illumination when energized at midnight.

CRI 95+ LEDs

Uncompromising color rendering eliminates sick grayish undertones, making textiles and foods appear naturally vibrant.

Flicker-Free Drivers

Zero stroboscopic pulsation prevents optical fatigue, migraines, and awkward video call interference.

Thermal Heat Sinks

Custom extruded aluminum heatsinks pull heat away from delicate glass and crystals, guaranteeing 50,000+ hour lifespans.
