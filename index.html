Design Principles

THE THREE LAYERS OF ARCHITECTURAL ILLUMINATION.

A truly luxurious interior does not rely on a single overhead bulb. It is composed of three orchestrations working in harmonic unison.

01

Ambient Lighting

The foundational base layer. Provides uniform, comfortable overall luminescence that replaces daylight without casting harsh, unflattering facial shadows.

Key Fixtures: Chandeliers & Coves
02

Task Lighting

Direct, concentrated illumination focused on designated functional areas such as dining tables, kitchen preparation counters, reading chaises, and desks.

Key Fixtures: Linear Pendants & Reading Lamps
03

Accent Lighting

The sculptural drama. High-intensity narrow beams and soft sconce halos that emphasize paintings, textured stone feature walls, and bespoke collectibles.

Key Fixtures: Wall Sconces & Spotlights
