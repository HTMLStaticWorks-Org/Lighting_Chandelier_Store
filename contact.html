Showroom Policies

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS.

Essential information regarding salon visits and purchasing.

Walk-in guests are welcome Monday through Saturday between 10:00 AM and 7:30 PM. However, to guarantee dedicated time with a senior lighting architect and private access to our dimming simulation gallery, we strongly recommend pre-booking an appointment using the form above.

Yes. Over 40% of our production comprises bespoke commissions. We routinely adjust chandelier diameters, drop lengths for double-height ceilings, metal finishes, and glass tint formulations to suit your interior palette.

Certainly. We partner with hotel groups, boutique developers, and architectural firms for volume procurement. Our trade program provides tiered commercial pricing, custom photometric IES files, and dedicated technical project managers.

Yes. For local Mumbai, Pune, and Delhi NCR projects, our field lighting consultants can conduct site visits to inspect structural ceiling reinforcements, verify clearance heights, and collaborate with your electrical contractors.

Signature collection items in standard finishes are maintained in limited stock at our Mumbai distribution hub and ship within 5–7 business days. Custom or made-to-order crystal chandeliers typically require 4–8 weeks for hand assembly and optical testing.
