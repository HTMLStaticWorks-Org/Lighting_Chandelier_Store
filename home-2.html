THE DETAILS THAT MATTER.

Hover or select each material component to inspect the meticulous finishing that defines LUMORA fixtures.

Purity Grade

Optical K9 Lead-Free Crystal

Cut with computer-guided diamond wheels and hand-buffed to achieve absolute transparency. When the internal warm LED illuminates through the facets, it produces crisp refractive highlights without color abberation.

  • Refractive Index: 1.516 for pure rainbow spectral dispersion.
  • Hardness: Superior scratch and clouding resistance.
  • Safety: Lead-free environmental formulation.

Artisan Glassblowing

Mouth-Blown Fluted & Opal Glass

Free-blown at 1,200°C by generational glassmakers. Each shade retains organic variations in wall thickness, diffusing light with soft, velvety warmth that machine-pressed glass can never recreate.

  • Forming: Individual wooden moulds and breath control.
  • Finish: Acid-etched matte opal or high-lustre fluting.

Metallurgy

Hand-Brushed Champagne Brass

Substantial solid cast brass hand-brushed with directional grit paper and sealed with an ultra-thin aerospace matte lacquer. Retains rich metallic warmth while preventing tarnishing and greasy fingerprint marks.

  • Composition: 65% copper, 35% zinc architectural grade.
  • Aging: Stable champagne gold tone that preserves luster.
